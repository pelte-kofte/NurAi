# Design Direction

## Visual Language
- Warm matte ivory
- Beige
- Muted sage
- Soft gold

## Style
- Islamic editorial calm style
- Tactile, quiet, warm, readable
- Designed for spiritual ease, not spectacle

## Avoid
- No glassmorphism
- No shiny gradients
- No neon
- No generic AI glossy UI

## Imagery
- Use images carefully.
- Images must support atmosphere without reducing legibility.
- Avoid decorative overload.

## Readability
- Screens must stay readable.
- Calm contrast is preferred over dramatic contrast.
- Typography and spacing should feel editorial and breathable.
- Motion should be minimal and meaningful.

## Interaction Principle
- Calmness before cleverness.
- Clarity before novelty.
- Spiritual intimacy before visual trendiness.
