# Copy Guide

## Core Direction
- Islamic wording over generic mindfulness.
- Prefer `tefekkür`, `zikir`, `dua`, `kalp`, `Allah` where appropriate.
- English copy should be calm and Islamic but not awkward.

## Avoid
- Avoid `universe`.
- Avoid generic `mindfulness` framing.
- Avoid `meditation app` vibe.
- Avoid wellness clichés that weaken Duada's identity.

## Tone
- Calm
- Warm
- Respectful
- Spiritually grounded
- Human, not preachy

## Turkish Guidance
- Keep Turkish natural and gentle.
- Prefer spiritually familiar language over startup/app jargon.

## English Guidance
- English should sound peaceful and confident.
- Avoid literal translations that feel stiff.
- Keep Islamic meaning intact without sounding ornamental.

## Product-Specific Reminder
- Reflection and Companion copy must feel intimate and safe.
- Premium copy must stay honest and must not imply features that do not exist.
