# Product Context

## Identity
- Duada is a calm Islamic spiritual companion.
- It is not a generic meditation app.
- It is not a social app.
- It is not a productivity app.

## Core Promise
- Help the user return to remembrance, calm, prayer, reflection, and Quran with warmth and clarity.
- Support spiritual rhythm across the day without noise, pressure, or gimmicks.

## Core Modules
- Home
- Prayer Times
- Quran
- Juz Journey
- Tasbih
- Asmaul Husna
- 1 Minute Reflection
- Stay With Me
- Widgets
- Premium
- Settings

## Product Tone
- Gentle, grounded, calm, and spiritually respectful.
- Emotional support should feel Islamic, not therapeutic-app generic.
- Utility features should remain spiritually coherent with the rest of the app.

## Feature Boundaries
- Home is an entry point, not a dumping ground.
- Premium should deepen access, not distort the core spiritual experience.
- Widgets and notifications should support the app calmly, not nag the user.
